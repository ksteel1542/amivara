# AMIVARA: Logo & Brand Identity Brief

**Stage:** 2 (Brand & UI Prototypes) · **Status:** Brief v1.0, ready for designer · **Owner:** Founder
**Inputs:** [AMIVARA_ROSE.md](../brand/AMIVARA_ROSE.md) · [tokens.json](../brand/tokens.json) · [UI_WIREFRAMES.md](../../docs/UI_WIREFRAMES.md)

## 1. The job of the logo

AMIVARA is an 18+ dating platform where **every member is a verified adult** and **every conversation is mutual**. The identity has to tell a first-time visitor in Lagos or Abuja, in under a second:

> *"This is warm, this is grown-up, and this is safe."*

### 1.1 Vibe: the three words

| **Warmth** | **Verified trust** | **Organic romantic elegance** |
|---|---|---|
| Human, inviting, a little golden-hour glow | Calm confidence, a seal of quality, nothing anxious | Flowing curves, editorial craft, romance with restraint |

### 1.2 Guardrails

| ✅ It should feel like | ❌ It must not feel like |
|---|---|
| A handwritten invitation to an evening you're looking forward to | A nightclub flyer or a hook-up app |
| A boutique hotel or a fine jewellery house | A bank, a telco, a fintech or a government ID agency |
| Grown, assured, contemporary African | Childish, cartoonish, bubbly or "cute" |
| Romantic in its curves | Sexual or suggestive in its shapes |
| Crafted, with slight organic irregularity | Cold geometric tech or a generic SaaS wordmark |

**Banned motifs:** hearts (including hidden or abstract hearts), flames, lips, kiss marks, Cupid or arrows, wedding rings, padlocks, shields, **check marks** (reserved for the verified badge, see §2.4), speech bubbles, map pins, stars made of five points, flags or national clichés, and gradients that imitate Instagram or Tinder.

## 2. Concept direction

### 2.1 Core idea: "Two curves meeting"

The **A** of AMIVARA is drawn as **two organic curves that rise from separate points and meet at the apex**. That's mutual connection: two people choosing each other. A **small Champagne Gold point** at the meeting place is the "spark" where it becomes mutual, and it also quietly echoes the gold of verification.

### 2.2 Directions to explore (the designer should present all three in round 1)

| # | Direction | Description | Risk to manage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Meeting Arcs** *(lead)* | The A is two calligraphic, contrasting-stroke curves (a nod to Playfair's high-contrast serifs). They touch at the apex, which carries a gold point. The crossbar is left out or hinted at with a hairline. | Must read as an "A" at 16px, not as an abstract tent |
| **B** | **Ribbon A** | One continuous ribbon-like stroke that loops to form the A, like a single thread tying two people together. | Must not become a heart or an awareness ribbon |
| **C** | **Wordmark-led** | No separate symbol. A refined custom wordmark with a distinctive **"VA" ligature** or a gold point on the **I**. The monogram is taken from the wordmark's A. | The icon may be weaker without a strong standalone symbol |

### 2.3 Wordmark

- "AMIVARA" in caps, based on **Playfair Display SemiBold (600)**, then **custom-drawn**: refine the A, M and V, and adjust kerning and stroke contrast so the result is a unique, trademarkable mark rather than plain set type.
- Letter-spacing starts at +6% and is tuned by eye. Optical kerning only.
- Playfair Display is licensed under SIL OFL 1.1, and outlined artwork derived from it may be used in the logo. The final deliverable is **outlined vector paths, never live text**.
- Sentence-case "Amivara" isn't used in the logo. It's only for running text.

### 2.4 Relationship with the verified badge (important)

The **verified badge** (a gold rosette with a night check mark, defined in AMIVARA_ROSE §5) is a **product signal**. Only verified members get it. The logo must **not** look like that badge, or the trust signal is weakened.
- No rosettes, seals or scalloped circles in the logo.
- No check marks.
- Gold in the logo is limited to **one small point**, never a full gold fill of the mark (except the single-colour gold foil variant for print, §3.2).

## 3. Deliverables

### 3.1 Primary logo

| Variant | Layout | Colours (light canvas) | Colours (dark canvas) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Horizontal lockup** *(primary)* | Monogram to the left of the wordmark, monogram height = 1.6 × cap height | Wordmark and monogram **Burgundy `#52182F`**, apex point **Champagne Gold `#D4AF37`**, on **Warm Ivory `#FDFBF7`** | Wordmark and monogram **Warm Ivory `#FDFBF7`**, apex point **Champagne Gold `#D4AF37`**, on **Burgundy-Black `#1A0B12`** |
| **Stacked lockup** | Monogram centred above the wordmark | Same as above | Same as above |
| **Wordmark only** | Wordmark alone | Burgundy on Ivory | Ivory on Night |
| **Monogram only** | The A symbol | Burgundy and gold on Ivory | Ivory and gold on Night |
| **Hero-reversed** | Any lockup | Ivory and gold on **Burgundy `#52182F`** (the onboarding hero) | n/a |
| **Deep Rose accent** | Any lockup | **Deep Rose `#88304E`** mark, with the apex point **Ivory** (never gold on rose), on Ivory | Not used. Deep Rose isn't allowed in dark mode (2.34:1) |

Contrast check (from tokens.json):
- Burgundy on Ivory: 13.28:1
- Ivory on Night: 18.46:1
- Gold on Burgundy: 6.53:1
- Gold on Night: 9.07:1
- Deep Rose on Ivory: 7.88:1

The gold apex point on **Ivory** (2.03:1) is acceptable **only because it's a decorative accent and not the shape that identifies the logo**. The logo must still read clearly with the point removed.

### 3.2 Monochrome variants

| Variant | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Mono Burgundy | `#52182F` | Single-colour print, embossing, documents |
| Mono Ivory / white | `#FDFBF7` or `#FFFFFF` | Over photography (with a scrim), dark merchandise |
| Mono Black | `#000000` | Fax, newspaper, legal filings, NIPO trademark application |
| Gold foil | `#D4AF37` or a metallic foil spec | Premium print only (invitations, event signage). Never on screen as the main logo |
| **Android themed icon** | Alpha only (a single opaque layer) | Android 13+ themed icons. The system tints it. See §4.4 |

Rules: in mono variants the apex point becomes the **same colour** as the mark, not a cut-out, so the mark stays one solid shape.

### 3.3 Clear space & minimum sizes

- **Clear space:** at least the height of the wordmark's **"A" cap** on every side. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) goes inside it.
- **Minimum sizes:**

| Asset | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 96px wide | 25 mm |
| Wordmark only | 80px wide | 20 mm |
| Monogram | 16px (favicon) | 6 mm |

- Below 24px, use the **simplified monogram** (§5.3), which drops the hairline details and makes the apex point 15% larger.

### 3.4 Misuse (show these in the guidelines PDF)

Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows or glows, recolour outside the approved palette, put gold on Deep Rose, put the logo on busy photos without a scrim, rearrange the lockup, or set "AMIVARA" in live text as a stand-in for the logo.

## 4. Android app icon

> **Note on the reference frame:** **360 × 800dp** is the phone **screen** reference frame used in the wireframes, not the icon size. The icon is specified on Android's own **108 × 108dp adaptive icon** canvas below. The 360 × 800dp frame is used to **preview** the icon on a launcher home screen and for the splash screen (§5).

### 4.1 Concept

The **monogram alone**, in Warm Ivory, centred, with the Champagne Gold apex point, on a **Burgundy `#52182F`** background.

> ⚠️ **Decision for the founder:** AMIVARA_ROSE §8 originally said the icon background would be **Deep Rose `#88304E`**. That conflicts with the brand rule "never gold on rose". This brief recommends **Burgundy**:
> - It keeps the gold apex point (6.53:1 on burgundy).
> - It looks deeper and more premium.
> - It stands apart from the bright red, pink and orange icons common on Nigerian home screens.
>
> The designer should also present a **Deep Rose alternative with an ivory apex point** for comparison.

Optional: a very subtle radial lift (Burgundy `#52182F` at the edges to about `#6A2440` behind the mark) for depth. It must stay flat-looking and is never glossy.

### 4.2 Adaptive icon (Android 8.0+ / API 26+)

| Layer | Canvas | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | 108 × 108dp | Solid `#52182F` (or the subtle radial). No artwork near the edges, because launchers crop and parallax this layer |
| **Foreground** | 108 × 108dp | Ivory monogram and gold point, **inside the 66dp-diameter safe zone**, with the visual weight centred in about 48–56dp |
| **Monochrome** (Android 13+) | 108 × 108dp | The monogram silhouette with the apex point merged in, inside the 66dp safe zone. Alpha only |

- Test under **circle, squircle, rounded-square and teardrop** masks, and with launcher parallax (the layers move up to 18dp).
- File: `res/mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml` (with `ic_launcher_round.xml` the same), pointing at `@drawable/ic_launcher_background`, `@drawable/ic_launcher_foreground` and `@drawable/ic_launcher_monochrome`. Each is a **VectorDrawable**.

### 4.3 Legacy icons (API 24–25, required by minSdk 24)

These are PNG mipmaps exported with the shape pre-applied (rounded square plus a circular `ic_launcher_round`):

| Density | Scale | Launcher icon (48dp) | Adaptive layer (108dp), for reference/raster fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| ldpi | 0.75× | 36 × 36px | 81 × 81px |
| mdpi | 1× | 48 × 48px | 108 × 108px |
| hdpi | 1.5× | 72 × 72px | 162 × 162px |
| xhdpi | 2× | 96 × 96px | 216 × 216px |
| xxhdpi | 3× | 144 × 144px | 324 × 324px |
| xxxhdpi | 4× | 192 × 192px | 432 × 432px |

> ldpi is effectively obsolete. Ship it only if the build tooling generates it automatically.

### 4.4 Google Play assets

| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| Hi-res icon | **512 × 512px**, 32-bit PNG, sRGB, ≤ 1024 KB. Full-bleed square, because **Play applies the corner mask itself** (don't pre-round it or add shadows) |
| Feature graphic | 1024 × 500px. The horizontal lockup on a Burgundy hero, with golden-hour photography. No text other than the logo and at most one tagline |

### 4.5 Small-size legibility tests (required)

The designer must show the icon at real size on a 360 × 800dp home-screen mock-up (light and dark wallpaper), at **48dp, 36dp (notification-adjacent) and 24dp**, and alongside WhatsApp, Instagram, Opay and Tinder icons, to check it stands out without shouting.

### 4.6 Notification (status-bar) icon

- `ic_stat_amivara`: a **24 × 24dp, single-colour white silhouette** of the simplified monogram on a transparent background, kept within 20 × 20dp. Android ignores colour here, so it's a pure silhouette.
- The notification accent colour is set to `#88304E` (light) / `#E8A5BC` (dark).

## 5. Splash screen mark

### 5.1 Behaviour

- Uses the **Android 12+ SplashScreen API**, back-ported to API 24–30 through `androidx.core:core-splashscreen` (configured with `flutter_native_splash`).
- **Static** at launch for the MVP. An optional Animated Vector Drawable (the two curves drawing in and meeting, then the gold point appearing) can be added later, **≤ 1000ms**, and must respect "Remove animations".
- The splash is shown only until Flutter's first frame. It's **never a deliberate delay**.

### 5.2 Spec (on the 360 × 800dp reference frame)

| Element | Light mode | Dark mode |
|---|---|---|
| `windowSplashScreenBackground` | Warm Ivory `#FDFBF7` | Burgundy-Black `#1A0B12` |
| Icon (`windowSplashScreenAnimatedIcon`) | Burgundy monogram with a gold point, **no icon background** | Ivory monogram with a gold point, no icon background |
| Icon canvas | **288 × 288dp** canvas, with the artwork inside the **192dp-diameter** circle (the system masks outside it). Visual weight about 120–144dp | Same |
| Branding image (optional, `windowSplashScreenBrandingImage`) | The wordmark, Burgundy, **200 × 80dp** canvas, near the bottom | Ivory wordmark |
| Position | Centred. On a 360 × 800dp frame the mark sits at about y = 400dp | Same |

> If an icon background is ever used on the splash (`windowSplashScreenIconBackgroundColor`), the icon canvas shrinks to 240dp with a 160dp mask. That's not recommended: the clean ivory or night canvas is more elegant.

- Flutter's first screen (Welcome, Screen 1) **starts on the same background colour**. On light mode, the Burgundy hero then fades in, so the move from the system splash to the Welcome screen is seamless with no flash.

## 6. Vector & SVG production guidelines

### 6.1 Master artwork

- Draw on a **24-unit grid** for the monogram (it scales to 24, 48, 108 and 288 cleanly) and a baseline and cap-height grid for the wordmark.
- Master files in Figma (preferred) or Illustrator → `design/logos/source/`.
- All shapes are **closed, filled paths**: **convert strokes to outlines**, outline all text, and use boolean unions so each colour is one compound path.
- Keep anchor points to the minimum: smooth curves with no stray or overlapping points. Target fewer than about 60 nodes for the monogram.
- **Optical corrections:** the apex point must sit visually centred (allow overshoot), and curves should overshoot the baseline and cap height by about 1–2%.

### 6.2 SVG export rules (web and source for Android conversion)

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| `viewBox` set, **no fixed `width`/`height`** on the root (set them in CSS or JSX) | Scales cleanly to any size |
| Artboard trimmed to the artwork plus the defined clear space (one version with clear space, one trimmed) | Predictable alignment in headers |
| Only `<path>`, `<g>` and solid `fill`. **No** `<text>`, `<image>`, raster embeds, filters, blend modes, masks or `<style>` blocks | Android VectorDrawable can't use them, and they bloat files |
| Colours as hex values matching the tokens. For a single-colour web version use `fill="currentColor"` so CSS can theme it | Keeps the logo in step with tokens.json |
| Optimise with **SVGO** (`--multipass`), keeping `viewBox` and removing metadata and editor cruft | Target under 4 KB for the monogram and under 10 KB for the lockup |
| Add `role="img"` and `<title>AMIVARA</title>` (web inline) | Accessibility |
| No gradients in the primary logo. The optional icon background radial is the only gradient allowed | Clean scaling, small files |

### 6.3 Android (VectorDrawable)

- Convert from SVG with **Android Studio → Vector Asset**. Check the XML by hand afterwards.
- Gradients (`<gradient>` inside `aapt:attr`) are supported from **API 24**, which matches our minSdk, but keep them to the icon background only.
- Keep `pathData` compact (Lint warns on very long paths, which affect startup rendering). Keep the viewport at **108 × 108** for icon layers and **288 × 288** for the splash icon.
- Render tests: the designer and developer must check the icon visually at **ldpi through xxxhdpi** using the Android Studio preview and a physical low-end device (for example a Tecno or itel phone at hdpi).
- Raster PNG exports (legacy mipmaps and Play) are produced **from the vector master**, never by upscaling a smaller PNG.

### 6.4 Next.js web

| Asset | Spec | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Header logo | Inline SVG React component (`<AmivaraLogo variant="horizontal" />`) using `currentColor` for the mark and a CSS variable for the gold point. **24–32px tall** on mobile, **32–40px** on desktop | `apps/web/components/brand/` |
| Dark-mode header | Same component. The colour switches via `--am-text` / `--am-accent` (tokens) with `prefers-color-scheme` or `data-theme`. **No second image request** | — |
| Favicon (SVG) | `icon.svg`, the simplified monogram, with an embedded `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` style so it shows Ivory on dark browser tabs | `apps/web/app/icon.svg` |
| Favicon (fallback) | `favicon.ico`, 16 + 32 + 48px multi-size | `apps/web/app/favicon.ico` |
| Apple touch icon | 180 × 180px PNG, full-bleed Burgundy background (Safari on web users' iPhones) | `apps/web/app/apple-icon.png` |
| PWA / manifest | 192 × 192 and 512 × 512 PNG, plus **maskable** 512 × 512 with the mark inside the central 80% safe zone | `apps/web/public/icons/` |
| Open Graph / social | 1200 × 630px. The horizontal lockup on a Burgundy hero | `apps/web/app/opengraph-image.png` |
| Performance | Header SVG inlined (no layout shift), with no font loading needed for the logo, because it's outlined paths | — |

## 9. Legal & ownership

- **Trademark search and filing** with the **Nigerian Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry (NIPO)** in **Class 45** (dating and social introduction services) and **Class 9** (downloadable mobile apps) before the final logo is announced. Consider Classes 38 and 42 later.
- The designer's contract must **assign all IP and copyright** in the logo, the source files and any rejected concepts to AMIVARA, with no portfolio use until after launch.
- The final mark must be **original**: the designer confirms they haven't used stock, AI-generated or template elements.

## 10. Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Reads as "A" / "AMIVARA" at 16px (favicon) and 24dp (notification)
- [ ] Still recognisable with the gold point removed and in single-colour black
- [ ] Doesn't look like the verified badge, and has no hearts, check marks, shields or padlocks
- [ ] Uses only AMIVARA ROSE tokens. Never gold on Deep Rose. Never Deep Rose on a dark background
- [ ] Adaptive icon foreground sits within the 66dp safe zone, tested under 4 masks and with parallax
- [ ] Monochrome themed icon and white status-bar icon supplied
- [ ] Splash icon within the 192dp circle on the 288dp canvas, in light and dark
- [ ] SVGs: `viewBox`, no `<text>`, filters or rasters, SVGO-optimised, under the size targets
- [ ] VectorDrawables render correctly from ldpi to xxxhdpi and on a physical low-end device
- [ ] Next.js header component themes through tokens with no extra requests
- [ ] Founder sign-off on the "warm, grown-up, safe" test results
- [ ] NIPO trademark search clear
